what it is

A dependency graph of tasks stored as plain text. Dependencies are edges. Completing a task unblocks downstream work. A coordinator service dispatches AI agents to ready tasks. Cycles let long-running processes loop with bounded iteration. The graph is the program.

.workgraph/graph.jsonl — git-friendly, no server, no account.

cycles

Processes that need to repeat — CI pipelines, monitoring checks, convergence loops — are expressed as cycles in the graph. Each cycle has a bound. Agents pick up where previous iterations left off.

run-tests  (open) ↺ (iter 0/5) ◀────────┐
└→ fix-failures  (open)                   │
  └→ push-check  (open) ────────────────┘

organizational synthesis

Agents have roles (what they do) and motivations (constraints on how). After each task, agents are evaluated. An evolver mutates and recombines the best performers. The organization restructures itself based on what works.

graph structure

Fan-out decomposes work. Fan-in synchronizes it. Arcs on the right show convergence — multiple paths feeding into one task.

coordination

$ wg init
$ wg add "Design the API"
$ wg add "Build backend" --after design-the-api
$ wg add "Write tests" --after build-backend
$ wg service start --max-agents 3

The service dispatches agents to ready tasks, evaluates results, evolves the agent population, and loops. You define structure. It does the rest.
